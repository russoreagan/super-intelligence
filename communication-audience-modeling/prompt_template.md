# Audience Modeling Prompt Template

## Objective
To dynamically profile the target audience and adapt communication output based on their characteristics.

## Steps
1. Identify key dimensions of the audience (demographics, psychographics, behavior).
2. Formulate profiling questions for each dimension.
3. Establish a scoring mechanism to prioritize dominant traits.
4. Generate an 'Audience Profile Card' with recommendations for tone, format, vocabulary level, and channel selection.

## Example Questions
- Demographic: What is the age range of your audience?
- Psychographic: What are their values and interests?
- Behavioral: How do they typically engage with content?

## Scoring Mechanism
Use a weighted system to score responses. Higher scores indicate more significant influence on communication strategy.
